My Love mine all mine ❤️

Hey Love, Happy Girlfriend's Day. Today is a celebration of you...😍My Rue Rue🥰 The Sun That Never Sets The last four months have felt like borrowing daylight from forever. Loving you is what I imagine the morning feels when it kisses the earth— warm, gentle, certain. You are my sun that never sets. Even when the sky grows dark, you leave enough light in my heart to find my way home. Your smile... It's dangerous. Not because it hurts, but because it heals. It spreads like dawn across my soul, and before I know it, I'm smiling too. Then there are your eyes. Those quiet, beautiful, midnight eyes. Soft enough to comfort me, deep enough to drown me. You look at me as if you're innocent, as if you don't know you're rewriting my heartbeat with every glance. I can't stare for too long. Because I'll disappear. I'll forget why I was angry. Forget what I wanted to say. Forget that time exists at all. The universe shrinks until it's just... you. And me. And the silence between our heartbeats. We don't always hold hands. Only when words can no longer carry what we feel. And when your fingers wander into my hair, when you pull me closer, when your breath catches for just a second— the whole world holds its own breath with us. In that moment, love speaks without saying a single word. And your laugh... Oh, your laugh. If joy had a voice, it would sound like you. If peace had a melody, I'd hear it every time you laughed. I'd choose that sound over every song ever written. If you were a movie, I'd call it Learning How to Love You. Not because love is difficult— but because you're a masterpiece, and masterpieces deserve a lifetime of study. I'd be the first one in, the last one out. I'd work mornings, afternoons, midnights, overtime, weekends, holidays. Not because I have to— because I never want to clock out. Call me a workaholic. No... A girlfriend-aholic. Hopelessly, beautifully, willingly addicted. Every heartbeat is another craving. Every goodbye is a withdrawal. Every hello is another dose of heaven. If loving you is a beautiful addiction, don't cure me. If your love is the sweetest poison, don't save me. Let me lose myself in your smile, your laugh, your eyes, your touch— again, and again, and again. Because if forever has a face, it looks like you. And if love has a home, I've already found mine. In you. My sun... that never sets. More than words can explain.. . Forever Yours ❤️
